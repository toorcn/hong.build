# How I build

## 0. think.build.test.break.correct.repeat

Speed is not the same as progress. I'd rather run ten cheap loops than one expensive guess.

## 1. intent beats volume

Some of my best work came from the smallest prompts. Some of my worst came from throwing everything at the wall. Unclear prompts are usually unclear thinking.

## 2. ai is infrastructure, not magic

After RM25,000 of AI, the first good answer impresses me least. What matters is what happens after: can I give it context, tell when it's making something up, and turn the output into something that actually works?

## 3. the tool is never the point

Whether it's a meeting moderator or a convention, the build only matters if the room works. I optimize for the person, then the system.
