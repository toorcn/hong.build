# HackMIT / HARE

A prototype robot learning companion for preschoolers, built at HackMIT 2026.

- sep 2026 — HackMIT 2026: #1 Hardware — Special Prize (HARE — a prototype robot learning companion for preschoolers.)

## What if the lesson adapted to the child?

### The question came first

Some of us had volunteered to teach children with ADHD. We'd seen that one way of teaching didn't work for every child. At HackMIT, that experience became a question we could build around: could we give children different ways to reach the same learning goal?

### Learning through physical play

We built HARE — Hands-on Adaptive Real-world Education — a prototype learning companion for preschoolers. Voice interaction, an expressive face and a robot dog brought the lesson into the room. We wanted to explore learning through movement and play, beyond a screen.

### First, get it to stand

Before the weekend, I didn't know what we'd build. We changed direction, worked with the robot and dimOS for the first time, and strapped parts together near the end. Getting it to stand and respond was part of the work. Showing HARE to people meant learning how to explain it to different listeners while keeping the reason we built it at the centre.

### A small experiment, worth starting

HARE won a hardware prize at HackMIT. I turned 22 that weekend, too. The prototype was one small experiment in an idea I care about: children deserve more than one way to learn. We didn't have the whole answer when we started building.
