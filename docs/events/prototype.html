Branch only where it's needed, floating platform display

The same phone shown twice, in normal and night service, and both fully interactive. Scroll or drag the train to Tue 17, Fri 20 and Mon 30 to see the branches.

① Branches only on the dates that need them. On a day with a Singapore or Online event, a teal branch curves off the main line, runs to that day's stations and ends there. The day's station gets a teal ring to show it connects. When the red train arrives, a little teal train runs out along the branch. Online-only branches are dashed. The rest of the right side stays empty.
② The platform display floats. The map now fills the whole screen, and the display hovers over it with a soft amber glow and frosted glass, so stations scroll underneath it. The header fades into the map instead of sitting in a box.
